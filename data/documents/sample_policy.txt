Company Refund Policy

1. Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase.
The product must be in original condition with all packaging intact.

2. Process
To initiate a refund, contact customer support via email at support@company.com.
Include your order number and reason for the refund request.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

3. Exceptions
Digital products and customized items are non-refundable.
Sale items may only be exchanged, not refunded.

4. Technical Support
For technical issues, contact our 24/7 support team.
We may offer troubleshooting before processing a refund.
